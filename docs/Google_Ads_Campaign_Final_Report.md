# Google Ads Campaign Final Report – SkillBoost Academy

## 1. Executive Summary

SkillBoost Academy is an imaginary online learning platform created for an academic Google Ads capstone. The project demonstrates how to plan a Google Search Campaign aimed at generating qualified leads and course registrations.

**Important:** This is a simulated/practice campaign. Any performance numbers are simulated examples and are not actual Google Ads results.

## 2. Business Overview

SkillBoost Academy offers practical technology courses in Java, Python, AWS, DevOps and Web Development. The proposed tagline is **Learn Skills. Build Projects. Grow Your Career.**

## 3. Campaign Objectives

- Generate qualified leads and course registrations.
- Increase relevant website traffic.
- Generate course enquiries and free-demo registrations.
- Build awareness among students and young professionals.
- Measure performance using CTR, CPC, conversion rate and cost per conversion.

## 5. Target Audience

Students, recent graduates and young professionals interested in programming, cloud computing, DevOps, IT careers, certifications and online education.

- Python Courses – Python programming and training

## 12. Performance Analysis

The simulated campaign generated 1,250 clicks from 25,000 impressions, producing a 5% CTR. With a simulated CPC of ₹20, the spend was ₹25,000. The campaign generated 100 conversions, resulting in an 8% conversion rate and ₹250 simulated cost per conversion.

## 13. Optimization Strategy

- Pause weak keywords and expand high-performing themes.
- Add irrelevant search terms as negative keywords.
- Test multiple headlines and descriptions.
- Shift budget toward valuable conversions at an acceptable cost.
- Improve landing-page relevance, mobile usability, speed and CTA visibility.
- Compare performance by location, age group and device.

## 14. Challenges Faced

The main challenges were keyword selection, avoiding irrelevant traffic, working within a limited budget, creating relevant ad copy and understanding the difference between clicks and meaningful conversions.

## 15. What I Learned

This project showed that effective Google Ads management requires more than creating advertisements. Keyword relevance, campaign structure, audience targeting, bidding, landing-page experience, conversion tracking and continuous optimization all contribute to campaign performance.

## 16. Recommendations

1. Conduct deeper keyword and competitor research.
2. Create dedicated landing pages for each course.
3. Review search terms and negative keywords regularly.
4. Test multiple ad variations.
5. Increase budget gradually for strong-performing ad groups.
6. Implement accurate conversion tracking before evaluating profitability.
7. Test Display, Video, Remarketing and Performance Max after sufficient data is available.

## 17. Conclusion

The SkillBoost Academy project demonstrates an end-to-end simulated Google Ads campaign from planning through performance evaluation. It provides practical understanding of search intent, keywords, ad creation, audience targeting, budgeting, conversion tracking and optimization.

**Final Campaign Message:** Learn Skills. Build Projects. Grow Your Career.
